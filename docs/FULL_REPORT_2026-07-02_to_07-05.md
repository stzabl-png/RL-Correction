## 2. The winning recipe (exact, reproducible)

Task `Isaac-Inhand-Rotate-Sharpa-Wave-GraspXL-BandRot-PoseBank-Net-v1` (v3net-p8: separate critic,
σ-floor 0.1, entropy 1e-3, H=32), resets from the Track-B adjusted pose (`n4_bank_final/pose1.npy`),
env-var knobs (all guarded, default-off, in `sharpa_wave_graspxl_bandrot_posebank.py`):

```
SHARPA_BAND_HELD=1            # income only while held (n>=2, disp<5cm)
SHARPA_BAND_FLOOR=0.06        # pay floor just above the 0.037 jitter attractor
SHARPA_BAND_SCALE=8.0
SHARPA_BAND_CEIL=1.2          # BAND-PASS: pay tapers to 0 above (anti-fling) + ratchet clamp
SHARPA_CENTER_SCALE=0.3       # tame the base centering annuity (was 1.0 = camp attractor)
SHARPA_BAND_NSCALE=3.0        # rotation pay scales with contact count (n/3)
SHARPA_BAND_EMA=0.85          # faster burst credit (0.9 underpaid short drive bursts)
SHARPA_SPARSE_THETA=0.15 SHARPA_SPARSE_BONUS=2.5   # finer ratchet grain

```
30M steps, 768–1024 envs, ~2 h on the 4090. Selection: **snapshot-sweep every `ep_*.pth`**
(`rl_rebuild/scripts/snapshot_probe.py`) and take the argmax — never trust `last.pth` or wandb
reward (§4). Draw variance: the harvested replicate reaches the strict tier reliably (day6 ep_400: held 0.92,
rate +1.04) — but the ENDURANCE tier comes deterministically from the pinch-mixed variant
(ENC_FRAC=0.5), not from draw luck. Recommendation: pinch-mixed + 2 draws + snapshot harvest.

## 3. How each day moved the program

**Thu 07-02 — regrip designs & network.** R4X reward matrix (4 designs × ball/cylinder): R4R
shed-recruit only ball arm to climb the gravity curriculum; ball-stall root cause = entropy floor ×
survival-only income; horizon exonerated (H=16 control); exemplar spread-grasp proven
config-reproducible, and σ-annealing identified as its suppressor. v3net built (separate
asymmetric critic, priv 23, σ floor) after the network audit.

**Fri 07-03 — measurement layer, Track B, the occupancy law, band-pass.** Closed-loop overnight
established held-gated instruments (escape-spin/toss/palm-cage exploits closed) and that held ~1
rad/s ball rotation exists in bursts. Track B delivered the n≥4 supported poses (5-finger all-≥1N;
4-finger equal-load), confirming "in physics it must work". Six braking curves established the
**occupancy law**: ω(n_contact) is monotone increasing — n=5 rotation is perfectly directional at
~1 rad/s; "extra fingers = brakes" is dead; the bottleneck is *occupancy* of high-n drive modes.
The income ladder (6 runs) then showed every equilibrium (2-finger rotate, clamps, tier-camping,
burst-fling) was the correct optimum of its full income stack — the base env's centering term paid
static holds 3× learnable rotation. The fix that finally priced the *interior*: the **band-pass**
(zero pay above a speed ceiling) → first +1.9 rad/s held-median rotation, 4-finger drive.

**Sat 07-04 — burst-pay, perturbation, harvest.** Night: burst-pay fix (EMA 0.85 + fine ratchet)
took rate from 0.15→0.94 by paying short drive bursts properly; churn law formalized (5/5
continuation attempts destroyed fast basins). Day: **object-force perturbation training became the
master key** — it doubled episode life (the drop-rate wall), *and* stabilized optimization
(perturb lineages improve monotonically; sharp non-perturb lineages self-destroy while wandb
reward climbs — reward rose 128→372 as true quality collapsed 0.88→0.09 held). Snapshot-harvest
(`snapshot_probe.py`) became the selection method; ~45 checkpoints evaluated.

**Sun 07-05 — censoring, the low-g question, the pipeline.** Your decisive question ("does it
sustain at low g?") led to the discovery that our 600-step eval windows had been **right-censoring
exactly the long episodes the goal asks about**: at 120-s windows the champion scores 10.1 rad/ep
mean at full gravity — the "wall" was one-third measurement artifact. Low-g itself proved to be a
*different task*, not an easier one (at g=−2 the ball floats out of the held/seat geometry;
pose-started training clamps) — **but the mixed pinch-reset variant passed all axes at −2 (8.5
rad/ep)**, because pinch starts force learning grasp construction and seat *recovery*. Applying
that at full gravity produced the final result: **fullg_adjrot, 21.8 rad/ep from 50% pinch starts.**
Recovery skill, not drop avoidance, is what buys endurance.

## 4. The six laws (strong evidence, replicated in-house)

1. **Pricing is destiny.** Every equilibrium observed across ~60 runs was the correct optimum of
   the *full* income stack (base env terms included). Audit the whole stack before blaming
   exploration. Conjunctions (hold × rotate) need structural pricing — a band-pass with floor AND
   ceiling — not level tuning; any pay monotone in instantaneous speed has a boundary optimum
   (camp or fling).
2. **The occupancy law.** On both objects, rotation speed rises monotonically with contact count
   and becomes perfectly directional at max n. Multi-finger modes are drive modes; make them the
   paid ones (n-scaled income).
3. **The churn law + harvest.** Fast-rotating basins are terminal snapshots under continued
   exploration-heavy training (5/5 destruction: continue, anneal, reprice-up, frozen-exploit).
   Perturbation training is the exception mechanism (stabilizes). Selection = snapshot sweep
   argmax; never `last.pth` by default.
4. **Training reward is not model selection.** It climbed while true quality collapsed. Only
   held-gated deterministic probes count.
5. **Eval windows censor.** Score episode-level goals with windows ≥2–3× the episode cap, or the
   best policies look mediocre (10.1 vs 3.9 on the same checkpoint).
6. **Reset diversity teaches recovery.** Pinch-mixed resets doubled endurance at full gravity and
   rescued low-g from the clamp. Guards and perturbations must be gated on the physics regime
   where they're meaningful (three separate zero-g incidents).
